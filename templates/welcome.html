Is this vintage Pyrex worth $8?
Yes — easy flip. Vintage Pyrex in good condition sells $25–$60 depending on the pattern. At $8 you're looking at solid margin. Check for chips before you buy.
What pattern is most valuable?
Gooseberry and Butterprint are the most sought after. Promotional patterns like Lucky Clover also do well. Old Orchard is common and sells lower.
